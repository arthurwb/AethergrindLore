*Annihilist*
* Destruction of aura in an area
* Spell disruption
* Mental disorientation
* Soul destruction

Etymology: 
* "an": comes from an old term for aura, anima.
* "nihil": meaning "nothing"

Annihilation is very simple: all Annihilation spells do is destroy aura. This can be use in various ways but the core of all Annihilation spells are the same. This can be used to remove aura from an area, preventing arcane spells from being cast. The most common use is to counter a spell or to deactivate a piece of technology. Beyond this Annihilation can be used in a similar way to Animation in that it can affect a person's mind, though it cannot introduce new thoughts, only disrupt or fully stop someone's thought process.

Annihilation has a bad reputation since it pretty much only exists to fuck with the things other people have made. It can be used assist in various machines and is even sometimes very important, oftentimes uses in failsafe systems. It can also be good in the sense that it can destroy dangerous magical effects, a Annihilation mage could stop an evoker from casting a spells which could level a city.

The main reason Annihilation is feared or even outright illegal in some places is due to its ability to damage a person's soul. While it is not specifically known what happens to a person's soul after they die, it is understood that all people have a unique soul that they grow and mold as they grow and change. A soul is a tangible thing, it is the aura that a person holds within their body, it is what mages who use soul mana draw from. Because of this Annihilation magic can be used to directly target a person's soul and destroy it. Missing a small amount of your soul is not inherently dangerous, it may cause some mental or physical discomfort, and a person can even exist after their soul is entirely destroyed. The issue is, should a person's soul be destroyed and their body is broken and they are killed, they have nothing to release back into the world. No one knows what happens to a soul after a person is killed but it is known that they go somewhere, many believe they travel through the gates of Arcadia or along the river of Acheron, and many hold onto this a hope for life after death. If a person's soul is destroyed there is nothing to pass on, they are gone, their body falls to the ground and they are forever stricken from the world.
