*Annimator*
* Condense aura
* Projection
* 6th sense
* Empower other spells
* Telepathy 

etymology:
* "an": comes from an old term for aura, anima.
* "matio": meaning "builder" or "shaper"

Annimation governs the alteration or movement of aura. Mostly you will see this as a person condensing aura into shields, all shielding augments and equipment rely on animation to produce the shield. Along with this, since all spells use aura in some way, this would allow a person to alter the flow of aura into the spells and while Animation cannot disrupt the flow of aura (that would be annihilation) it can strengthen it or allow the mage to sense the flow of aura.

Aura has an intrinsic link to all people's minds. The way arcane mana even works is by taking advantage of that link and controlling the aura around you through concentrated thought. This can also work in reverse where by carefully warping the aura around a person you can affect their state of mind or even control them in certain ways. It should be noted that this is different from Theurgy in that there is no domination, when a person is commanded with Theurgy their bodies are hijacked by some other force, Animation works in a much more subtle way, warping the aura around a person to make them begin to think things themselves or to slip in specific ideas or feelings. While you may not be able to tell a person to open a door for you with animation, you can make them feel much more kind and inclined to open the door for you themselves. 

**The Law**
While Animation cannot be completely outlawed, pretty much every jurisdiction has some kind of rule in place which punishes the use of Animation to alter a person's mind. Similar to how Evocation is not outright illegal but shooting a fireball at someone is, casting a shield is fine but casting a spell to make a judge more lenient during a trial is always very illegal. Often times misuse of Animation magic is more severely punished than the misuse of other spells due to the devious and subtle nature of the magic.
