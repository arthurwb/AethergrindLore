What is soul?

Physically (or rather aetherically), soul is no a thing on its own and is instead an aspect of a person or things being. Soul is more like an adjective than it is a noun. Something can be wet, something can be hard, something can be big, and something can have an abundance of soul. Maybe think of it more the way we talk about music or art. A work can be made with or without "soul." If it is made with soul you can tell because it has some transcendent quality that can be sensed but not pinpointed. As one might expect, most living things have soul.

Soul is the way that all things interact with the aethereal plane. A person with an abundance of soul or a strong soul has a more significant impact and presence on the aethereal plane.

Soul is also the way that aura can be crafted into magic. The soul acts both as a valve and a reservoir. This valve aspect allow for a person to funnel aura from the aethereal plane, shape it, and then cast it as a spell. The reservoir allows a person to unleash their own font of aura. The latter being a much easier option, though it relies on a person's natural reservoir of aura, something that is understood to be determined at birth. The former can be trained though a person cannot train to have a larger reservoir of aura.

Arcane mages still make use of their soul as it is necessary for taking natural aura and bringing it into their minds and bodies to be shaped by their will and thoughts. Soul mages use their soul in same way, though they rely on their natural wellspring of aura rather than the aura found in nature.
