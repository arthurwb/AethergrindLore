Because magic requires extensive understanding of the world around a person and to be able to clearly explain basic concepts like location and self, personal runes provide a backbone for most mages in their casting.

A personal rune is a totally unique rune which a mage uses as a descriptor of themselves down to their very foundation. This along relative locational runes creates the foundation for spell casting, meaning a spell that is cast is cast relative to a person rather than in any random location is the universe.

Personal runes are one of the first things a mage is taught and serves as a badge of magery, marking their official status as a mage. Most people are very particular about their runes and put a lot of effort into designing or describing them.

Because runes are meant to describe a person, as a person grows and changes they are expected to update their rune to match. The process of updating a rune is an important and personal moment for mages, marking a significant change in their lives.
