**Conjuration**
* Moving things from on place to another
* Marking things to be moved
* Adjusting locality
	* Literally one of the principal underlying rules of physics. Breaks down all known laws. Significant.
	* Create locality, two objects that are not near each other can affect one another
	* Break locality, two objects that are close to each other cannot affect one another
	* Locality duplication, an object here is also somewhere else. Trippy.

**Transmutation**
* Alters particle identity
* While this can alter individual atoms, normally it alters a wide area of matter, changing a group of matter into something else. Essentially creating new protons, neutrons, and electrons from nothing or destroying them
* Cannot create new mass from nothing, needs a base. Can only add or subtract from existing mass
* Change material properties
	* density
	* hardness
	* elasticity
	* conductivity
* Restructure an area of mass. eg. healing and mending
