*Thaumaturge*
* Control natural forces
* Control non theic creatures
* Control plants

Thaumaturgy governs nature and things associated with nature. A thaumaturgical spell cannot effect a person or create energy and mass. The spells in this school do have a lot of overlap with other schools, but everything that a thaumaturgical spell should not be able to be done by another spell. Spells like change weather might be confused with transmutation, but it acts on a much wider scale, a transmutation spell requires a person to see the subject and understand the change that takes place. Alternatively thaumaturgy spells do not require complex knowledge and act on much broader scales. That said, they cannot do very specific things. While a transmutation spells can cause specific changes in a person or object, a thaumaturgy spell would not be able to do that.

Thaumaturgy spells also have a much broader area of spells and are not as locked down as some other spells school. That said they all focus on nature, so spells should always be affecting a force of nature and should be something that can happen naturally. A thaumaturgy spell would not be able to artificially cause a fission reaction in a non-radioactive rock.
