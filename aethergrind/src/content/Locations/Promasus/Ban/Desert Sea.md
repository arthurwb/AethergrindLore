The desert is a huge pile of quicksands, with underground pressurized aquifers pumping water up into the sand, loosening them and turning them into a kind of fluid. It does not rain in the desert (or rather rains very rarely) but rains almost constantly in the mountains which then pours down into the aquifers under the desert.

There are transient saline selves which form on the surface which are solid and wide enough for people to use to traverse the desert, though they are formed and destroyed by the aquifers meaning people have to keep careful study of the desert to know when there is a self and when there is not.

There are many animals and few plants which life in the lower layer of the desert, swimming through the quick sand. The most abundant and significant are sand planktons which feed off of the nutrients which wash in from the mountains and from decaying creatures in the sands. The most interesting of the creatures are huge slow moving hellbender like lizards which slowly move through the sands, filtering the sand and eating the planktons like a whale eats krill.
