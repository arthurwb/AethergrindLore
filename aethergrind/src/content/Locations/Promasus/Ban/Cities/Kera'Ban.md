Kera'Ban is the center of civilization with Ban.

Since it is almost impossible to create a true city in the desert due to the transitive nature of the dune sea, Kera'Ban was built as an alternative.

The many different groups which spent many years sifting the sands in Ban realized that it can be better to join together every now and then in order to gain protection from the dangers of the desert, trade wither each other, a move as a group to bring large quantities of unrefined iron to refineries along the edge of the desert.
