### Tradition of Strength
One of the fundamental tenants of Tallen culture is the Tradition of Strength. This tradition effectively dictates that strength, above all else, is the determining factor in all things. All disputes are settled via ritual combat. Leaders are either chosen for their feats of strength or because they were able to defeat the previous leader. Children born with traits which might cause them to grow to be weaker than other children are either sold into slavery or exiled.

Because strength is the only factor which decides a person's rank in Ye Tallo, the people do not care about nationality, race, or religion. If a foreigner were to come in prove they are stronger than a current leader, they will be treated with the highest respect. This is how Taro was able to maintain control over the region, he would simply need to visit every few months and kill the strongest guy in the country. Everyone would just nod their head and think "yup that guy is still the strongest."

Because of their general distain for technology and magic, a person using tech or magic in a fight is not seen as strong as it is believed that it is only the magic or tech which is strong, not the person. Because of this there is an odd subgroup of techno-fetishists who follow not the strongest person but instead what they believe to be the strongest machine or spell. These groups are looked down upon by Tallen civilization and often exiled for their beliefs. 
### Only War
While Ye Tallo has plenty of farms and can, for the most part, survive on their own, they often prefer to survive off of raiding Promasus. Because there is such a lack of farmland and because of the majority of the population living on the western coast, most of the country is untamed wilderness. All resources like livestock, ore and materials, and basic craft goods are all acquired via raiding the main land, meaning there are very few craftsmen or skilled laborers. 

Promasus has no trouble with sending a few families to die in the east, giving some minor noble cheap land so that they can get a few harvests in before they are eventually raided and killed, then replaced with some other minor noble. The Tallen see this more so as a challenge, they will continue to move west looking for things to fight and steal and if someone wants to keep putting things for them to fight and steal in their way then that's on them.

The only way that the Tallen have any chance against a modern nation like Promasus is because they are masters of both blood magic (which they view not a magic and instead just their own raw strength) and annihilation magic (which they view more so as a manifestation of their strength and power). Using this power they are able to create magic deadening walls and zones all across their land. When something crosses one of these walls or zones all magic is removed from the creature of object. Then, while a would-be invader is completely out of mana and non of the tools or weapons work, the Tallen storm in and overpower them with raw physical force. These zones and walls are created by the ritual construction of fortress temples, which server as either forward command centers or bulwarks. Should a fortress temple be destroyed the zone will slowly dissipate and aura moves back into the area.
