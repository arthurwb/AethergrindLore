1. Magistrate
	* A ruler of a large region, the highest station a person can hold in Kir culture.
2. Justiciar
	* A second in command to the Magistrate, often ruling a smaller territory within the rule of a local Magistrate.
3. Lord Sheriff
	* The hands of the Justiciar and the Magistrate, handling day to day operations of towns and villages as well as commanding the local guard.
4. Governance
	* All government officials which work directly for the Lord Sheriff, Justiciar, or Magistrate.
5. Soldiery
	* All levels of soldiers within the military. The Soldiery functions on its own hierarchy, though all members of the Soldiery exist in this level of the caste. A person is only a member of the Soldiery during their term of conscription.
6. Physicians
	* Originally just doctors, this category has since extended to any university trained person.
7. Artifex
	* An uneducated but still skilled laborer. Carpenters, scribes, machinists, mechanics, etc.
8. Laborer Majoris
	* Vague. Menial laborers who work jobs often considered more complicated than those worked by a Laborer Minoris. Often times a Laborer Majoris works a job which helps to create something. Factory assembler, brick layer, warehouse worker, construction worker.
9. Laborer Minoris
	* Vague. Menial laborers who work simple entry level jobs, often jobs that are necessary for the continued function of a certain process. A cog. Customer service, garbage collector, line cook, delivery person, etc.
10. Laity
	* An unemployed and uneducated person.
11. Dormitor
	* Any individual deemed unsuitable for normal work by the local government. Criminals, the mentally ill, drug addicts, etc.
