Heit'mazi has a history of thousands of years of war between the lutans who inhabit the mountains and the caelans who live on top of them. Thousands of years eventually lead to a generation of both caelans and lutans who desired peace between the two groups and after years of discussion and change of policy, the provisional government of Zek'el was formed. This would eventually expand into the entire country of Heit'mazi and the tenuous peace between the two groups would help to build the country and regions into the leading nation of north-western Vintus.
