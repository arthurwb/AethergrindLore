### Fauna
**Southern Thin-Fur Beaver**
* Similar to the beavers found north in Meer
* Thinner and more otter-like
* Longer flatter tails
* Thinner and shorter fur
* Longer breath holding (up to 2 hours)
* Almost entirely aquatic
* Eats mostly aquatic tree roots, such as the common tupelo and willow trees or mangrove trees further south
* Range is from middle-northern Listany to Escor
* Mostly nocturnal 
* Builds smaller semi-permanent hovels rather than dams which are mostly underwater
* Produce larger litters, though most are lost to predators before maturity
