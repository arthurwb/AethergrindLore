## Rooters
Monopoly: Stolen Good, Salvage

Rooters control most of the elevators around the Pit, charging for their use but also keeping track of who comes in and who comes out. Most of their money comes from buying loot from Janitors in the Pit and reselling it either in Buckethead or on the surface. Outside of this they are known to be chronic kleptomaniacs, stealing anything they don't already have. Because of this they have strained relationships with the other gangs, though the others are forced to work with them because of their control of the Pit.
## Casket Boys
Monopoly: Blood, Wetware

By far the most hated gang in Buckethead. The Casket Boys deal mostly in the buying and selling of corpses and blood. While they are known to sometimes go straight to the source and cut down someone with really nice looking eyes, more often than not they simply scavenge and grave rob. They say a casket boy is like a shark, the second any blood hits the water they start coming.
## Gunstreet Girls
Monopoly: Weapons

Born out of a need to protect themselves in the violence of Buckethead, originally the Gunstreet Girls were just a small collective stationed on Gun Street. Over time they muscled in on the arms trade and over time turn Gun Street into something more deserving of its name. The Gunstreet Girls are known for two major things: any men who work for them are never armed (though they often find work around with magic), and if anyone ever tries to muscle in on their business or turf they respond violently and sadistically. While they do not control the most area in Buckethead they are possibly the most significant.
## The Mosh
Monopoly: Violence, Entertainment

The Mosh originate from the underground music scene in the Promenade. While in the Promenade they were known for inciting riots under the guise of music performances. After they were listed as a threat to the city they had to move their operations down into Buckethead, applying their violent tendencies as thugs and extortionists, willing to work for anyone who needed a few heads caved in or to at least threaten as much. Now more of a side business, the Mosh still enjoys supporting local music, so long as it is fast and violent.
## Pharmacists
Monopoly: Drugs

The Pharmacists are the most organized group in Buckethead. Centralized out of the Pillbox they supply drugs for every part of the undercity, from Buckethead to the Promenade. Their gang is divided into a hierarchical system with 3 tiers. Producers work in the labs and the grow rooms where they synthesize the drugs. Distributors hit the streets and sell the drugs. Enforcers make sure the supply line runs efficiently. The Pharmacists are known for their brutal efficiency and being the most competent gang in Buckethead, though that may be because their industry requires people with at least a modest amount of intelligence.
