The land here is characterized by its abundance of rivers, most of which spawn from the hills along the coast and meet with the Kiren river as it reaches the sea. 

Mostly nestled along the coast along Clover Bay and near its northern and southern coast. Geographically the land east is considered Kir or Maldant.

The abundance of rivers, the bay, and the lack of storms found in the north and south makes Clover an oasis in the region.
