Home Stones are clumps of strange matter which form around eldritch or otherwise non-planer material, similar to how pearls form. The strange matter that forms is extremely valuable for its durability and conductive properties.

Home stones come in many different sizes, ranging from the size of a grain of sand to the size of a home.

Home Stones are very hard to crack, but if one is able to break it open then they can retrieve the object which caused the stone to form. Then, with wards or regular maintenance the stone can be prevented from forming again. Most Home Stones are formed by simple non-planer detritus, but some are formed by non-planer or weird relics of great power.

Strange matter can vary in its durability, with more durable matter often suggesting stronger sources of weird energy. Because of this there are some Home Stones which have been found but no one has been able to break into. Most are kept in Listany but many have been sold off to curio collectors or major governments.

Why "Home Stones"? The most common form of item which creates a Home Stone is not actually a physical object but instead a rift. These rifts are often 1 way
