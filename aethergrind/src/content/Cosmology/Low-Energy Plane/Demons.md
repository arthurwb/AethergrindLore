Demons are the inhabitants of the low energy plane. Acheron is a plane which encompasses all 

**Daemons**: Common lesser demons which can range in danger similar to a rat up to a drake.
**Knights**: Often simply Daemons that are able to command other daemons. Usually slightly more powerful.
**Dukes**: A lesser aspect of a King. Something like neglect, ennui, or guilt.
**Princes**: A larger aspect of a King. Something like regret, loneliness, or relaxation.
**Kings**: Represents a primary negative emotional aspect. Something like sorrow, peace, or contemplation.
