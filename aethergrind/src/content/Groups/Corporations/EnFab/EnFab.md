Originally named "Emerson Fabrication"

CEO is John William Emerson, who is officially alive and well but would be turning 210 this year and hasn't been seen in 50 years. Most assume that he died a while ago and they just don't want to say it or he is being kept alive in cryostasis or through extreme augmentation.

Headquarters in Kirhall, they own and manage most manufacturing centers in Vintus and many more in Promasus. Closely associated with Sigh Co. as they are responsible for the research and development for the items that their factories produce.
