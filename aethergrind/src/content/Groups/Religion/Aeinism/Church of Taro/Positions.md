### Ordo Custodiae
Cleric
- General purpose researcher. Usually someone working to become a Speculator, Malleator, or Acquisitor, or someone whose job is too broad to fit into one of those categories.
Speculator
- Focuses on the containment of objects
Malleator
- Focuses on the destruction of objects
Acquisitor
- Focuses on the retrieval of objects
