Road stations often had names beginning with Ad ("at" or "toward").

Examples:

Ad Decimum ("At the Tenth Mile")
Ad Fines ("At the Border")
Ad Aquas ("At the Waters")
Ad Flexum ("At the Bend")

These were especially common on human itineraries.
