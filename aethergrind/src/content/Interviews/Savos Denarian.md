What is your name?

My name's Savos Denarian. 

And where are you from Savos?

I'm from List Creek originally, but moved to Clover Station when I was a teen. List Creek's this tiny little village to the north, not quite on the border with Listany but its pretty close. Get all kinds of strange things coming down from those woods. List Creek's still around though, gots some family up there that I try to keep in contact with. They don't got the comms like we do, so we write.

What was growing up in List Creek like?

Oh well I guess it'd be just like growing up in any other of those small forgotten towns. They say its saintly to move to the city, that's what my parents said, but I remember the priest always telling us that we ought to set roots down where we can. The saints they had to set roots down. You think Clover Station was a big city when Dredon found the hill? I guess it would of been called Barlow then I guess? Well, no, he found a good hill and told the people to come. And they did. What's so different about our little creek, we aint helping nothing by running away from it. Our own little sanctuary. 

Of course, but what was growing up like?

Growing up in the country was nice. You learn a lot living in those hills. You learn a lot living in the city too, but its different things. In the hills you learn about nature, they teach the old magics out there, stuff about crops growing, don't got laws about changing the weather when there hasn't been any rain. My family was miners though, so a lot of our magics were about listening to the stone. Got a good mine up there, plenty of good copper. Aint gonna sell good but it puts food on the plate.

Did you have very much money when you were growing up?

Nah. Well, I guess that depends on what you mean by much. We had money enough to go to the store, to buy clothes, to pay for our house. I see people every day that can't do that in the city. But we didn't have money for any vans or anything fancy like that. You learn to live without that stuff. Wasn't till Clover that I even knew that we were one of the poorest town in the area, just felt like life livin in it. 

Why did your family end up moving to Clover Station?

My dad moved for work. Mine's good work but he had 6 kids and my ma at the time was real sick with some kind of bone disease and we didn't have the doctors to treat her right. Moved to New Barlow to just be closer to the doctors, put us kids in schools, and then hopefully carve out a new kinda life in there.

Did your family take to the city well?

Not really. There's a kinda stigma about hill people, specially for the Kir. I think everyone in Clover wanted to forget that were all river folk if you go back far enough. So, they were hard on us. My dad was able to find a job in a factory but they kicked him out once they figured out he lied about his going to school. So we scrapped by for a while. I was working age so I dropped out of school pretty quick to get a job. I was younger and was able to hide the drawl better than my dad so I got a line job in Barlow. My dad ended up in Buckethead, workin as hands in a kinda chop shop. He didn't talk about it much. My brothers did better though. Lot of them grew up in the city so they could take to it a lot better than me.

What happened to your mother, did she get the treatment she needed?

Yeah she did, for a while. Pretty much all the money that wasn't going to rent or food went to her doctors. They did what they could but she was too far gone even before we got to Clover. They put her in bed and got her all kind of medications. They said they could do some kind of surgery to fix her but we couldn't afford that. She ended up dying when I was sixteen.
