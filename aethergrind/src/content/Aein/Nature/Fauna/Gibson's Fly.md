(Floaters)

Gibson's Flies, or more commonly known as "Floaters", are aberrant creatures seemingly native to the Composite Plane though with no known range, appearing to be found anywhere there is an abundance of aura.

Floaters are not able to be perceived directly with most people only able to see them in their peripheral vision. Strangely, individual which are inebriated in some way are able to see Floaters much more clearly, usually this comes from intense drug or alcohol abuse or insomnia.

Floaters are described as having anywhere between 6 and 10 arms (legs?) with 2 fingered hands at their ends. Their arms connect to a central spherical body covered in hairs which are almost as long as their appendages. Their size can range anywhere from 1-10 millimeters Because of the hair and because of their elusive nature there is little else known about Floater's anatomy.

While Floaters can be found anywhere, they are found in more abundance in regions with more aura and, when in a confined space, they tend to move towards the outdoors. How Floaters move, how they sense aura, how they intrinsically know how to get outside, and how they might have found themselves inside a confined space are all still unknown.
