The Veen is a series of paths underground which connect all major luten cities in Vintus.

The Veen was created by a coalition of early lutens about 500 years ago as many strongholds started forming ties with their siblings across the continent. After about 100 years of work most of the Veen was completed. Among the Luten it is considered their greatest achievement and is cherished almost religiously with many luten even saying that the amount of effort put into the great work imbued it with a kind of divinity.

Because lutens revere the Veen so much it is often difficult for outsiders to gain access to it, and due to how deep in the bedrock the Veen sits, it is almost impossible to find an entrance that is not connected to a luten stronghold. Many have attempted to dig down to create their own entrance to the Veen but a coalition of luten nobles and rulers have been able to maintain their ownership of the Veen and were able to prevent anyone from digging too deep near the Veen.

For an outsider to gain access to the Veen they must first pay an entrance fee at any connected luten stronghold, after which they are given a writ of passage which allows them to travel to the next stronghold, from there they must repeat the process for each stronghold they pass through. Writes of passage are often extremely expensive but vary from stronghold to stronghold, with much of the cost of the writes being used to offset the extreme costs of claiming rights to such large areas of subterranean land across multiple national boundaries.

Much of the Veen sits in uncontested territory and falls under the same category that most of the roads connecting the polis fall under, which is that it is simply left up to the people to maintain those road ways if they wish to use them. There are no fees or taxes but that also means that no one patrols the roads or repairs them. For the above ground roads this is often left up to the porters, but for the Veen it is left up to crews of luten.

Along with being expensive to gain access too, the Veen is also very dangerous. Many strongholds will send patrols but those patrols only go so many miles out from the stronghold and mostly just serve as a means to make sure nothing creeps in from the Veen into the city. Most of the Veen is unguarded with entire sections becoming totally infested with all manor of creatures, often forcing strongholds to put out bounties for adventurers to clear those sections out.
