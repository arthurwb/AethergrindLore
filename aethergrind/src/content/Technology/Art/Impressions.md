An impression is similar to a photograph except instead of capturing light, a person's mind is imprinted onto a pieces of paper.

Impressionists are people who take these impressions and develop them. It takes a lot of training and expensive equipment but a trained impressionist is able to create entire scenes using only their thoughts.

The most practical use of this is similar to how we use photographs. Someone looks a subject, they activate the device, and in a moment the impression is loaded into the machine. From there the impressionist takes the time to develop the impression which is essentially printing the brain scan to a piece of paper (or whatever material they are printing to).

Untrained Impressionists will often have issues with their impressions not being entirely accurate and someone off the street trying to use an impressioning device will produce only blurry shapes.

Beyond this, very skilled Impressionists have been able to create entirely new images without a specific subject, or by altering the subject. This requires intense control over one's thoughts and takes years to master. Once mastered though, an Impressionist can create scene totally unique and profound. They could also employ their skills to create subtly altered impressions, something which is illegal in most jurisdictions.
