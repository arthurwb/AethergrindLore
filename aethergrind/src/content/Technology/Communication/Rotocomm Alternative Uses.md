Because rotocomms are so wide spread people have devised different uses for them.

A major use it to use them kinda like radios, where a 1 way receiving signal is sent and then as many people as the system enables can connect to it and hear it. This functions just like a radio where you can tune in to listen to music or a sports game or a debate or whatever. The main difference is that its not a seperate device, it is just connected to your phone, so you just dial the number for the radio station and connect, needing to dial it again if someone calls you or you want to stop listening. Called *Multibands*, these stations are extremly common and often reserve 4 digit signals, so if a normal person has an 8 digit signal like 5648-5465, a multiband would only be something like 6468, so youll have stations with funny names like 8317 the "BELT", a popular music station in Barlow in Clover Station, or 8453 the "BASE", the largest broadcasting network for baseball.
